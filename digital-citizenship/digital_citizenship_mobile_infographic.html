Types of Digital Users

Interactive mobile visual guide for quick revision, classroom learning, and exam prep.

Category 01

The Digital Native

Core Rule

Born AFTER widespread adoption of digital tech.

Children & young people who grew up from an early age using technology such as the internet, computers, and mobile devices.

3 Sub-Categories of Natives

Type 1

Avoiders

  • Do NOT feel any love or interest in technology.
  • Maintain mandatory emails only for essential obligations (school/work).
  • Do NOT use social media.
Disinterested
Type 2

Minimalists

  • Realize technology is a necessary part of today's world.
  • Engage minimally and only when strictly necessary.
  • Use search engines only if required; buy online only as last resort.
Practical Need
Majority Group

Enthusiastic Participants

  • Thoroughly enjoy and thrive on using modern tech & gadgets.
  • Technology is fully integrated into their daily routines & lifestyle.
Most Natives
Category 02

The Digital Immigrant

Core Rule

Born BEFORE widespread adoption of digital tech.

Includes individuals who were not exposed to digital technology at an early age and had to learn & adapt later in adult life.

3 Sub-Categories of Immigrants

Type 1

Avoiders

  • Prefer a lifestyle with minimal technology attachments.
  • Prefer traditional communication: landline phones and postal mail.
  • Resist shifting to digital methods.
Traditional
Type 2

Reluctant Adopters

  • Engage minimally and only when strictly necessary.
  • Use search engines if required; buy online only when no offline option exists.
Hesitant
High Tech

Enthusiastic Adopters

  • Can comfortably keep pace with Digital Natives due to high comfort & capacity.
  • Clearly see the value of tech and have a natural knack for learning gadgets.
Tech-Savvy

Side-by-Side User Comparison

Direct breakdown matching Digital Native profiles against Digital Immigrant profiles by engagement level.

Low / No Interest
Native: Avoider

Mandatory email only for school/work. Absolutely no social media. Disinterested in gadgets.

Prefers landline phones & postal mail. Minimal attachments to tech.

Need-Based Usage
Native: Minimalist

Cautious & hesitant; uses search engines or online buying only when no offline alternative exists.

Self-Test Study Flashcards

Tap cards to check answers

Test your Form 2 Information Technology digital user knowledge!

Question 1

What single factor determines whether someone is a Digital Native or Digital Immigrant?

💡 Answer: Whether they were born AFTER (Native) or BEFORE (Immigrant) the widespread adoption of digital technology.
Question 2

How does a Native Avoider differ from a Native Minimalist on social media?

💡 Answer: Native Avoiders do NOT use social media at all and have no interest in tech. Minimalists acknowledge tech is necessary in today's world and use it when strictly required.
Question 3

Which category of Digital Immigrant can comfortably keep pace with Digital Natives?

💡 Answer: Enthusiastic Adopters (they see technology's value and have a natural knack for learning gadgets).
Question 4

Name two traditional communication methods preferred by Immigrant Avoiders.

💡 Answer: Landline phones and postal mail.
