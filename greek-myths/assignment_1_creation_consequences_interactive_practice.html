Model Answers (Part B):

1 (3 marks): They had had enough fighting and wanted to divide the universe fairly without starting another argument.

2 (3 marks): Zeus: Heaven / the sky (with Mount Olympus as his royal home). Poseidon: The sea. Hades: The underworld.

3 (4 marks): Because the ancient Greeks believed Poseidon ruled the sea and Zeus ruled the sky, they explained ocean waves, storms, and lightning as the actions and moods of these gods ruling their kingdoms.

4 (4 marks): When Pandora opened the chest the first time, Troubles like Disease, War, Old Age, and Death escaped into the world. When she opened it a second time, Hope fluttered out and gave humans (and Prometheus) the courage and strength to bear those hardships.
