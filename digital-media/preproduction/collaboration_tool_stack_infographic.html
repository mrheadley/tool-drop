Form 6 Proposal Writing & Collaboration Rules

Essential rules for achieving high marks in Digital Media Unit 2

📌 1. Document Everything

Take screenshots of your Trello board, Miro mind maps, and Google Docs version history. Include these in your proposal appendix to prove active collaboration!

⚡ 2. Assign Clear Roles

Avoid "everyone does everything." Designate a Lead Researcher, UI Designer, Proposal Writer, and Media Manager on your Task Management board.

🔒 3. Maintain Folder Structure

Organize your Cloud Drive into folders: `/01_Research`, `/02_Proposal_Drafts`, `/03_Design_Assets`, and `/04_Final_Submission`. Name files with version numbers (e.g., `Proposal_v2.docx`).
